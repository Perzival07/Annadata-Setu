## Q&A Positioning
- **Vs Plantix**: Plantix does isolated visual image identification. AgriStack builds identity rails. Annadata Setu is farmer-sourced disease surveillance with pre-emptive neighbour alerts. We are the open advisory layer on top of AgriStack — complementary, not competing.
- **Privacy & k-Anonymity**: Clusters with $<5$ reports are never returned or stored with PII. All spatial queries use geohashes, not user identity.
